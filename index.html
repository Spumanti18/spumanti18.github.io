ClinchClips

Short-form UFC and MMA moments on YouTube

ClinchClips is a YouTube Shorts channel, @ClinchClipsTV, that posts short clips of funny, interesting and newsworthy moments from the UFC and MMA world: podcast moments, interviews, press conferences and fighter highlights.

About the ClinchClips application

The ClinchClips application is a private tool used only by the channel owner to manage the ClinchClips YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload Shorts to the ClinchClips channel and schedule when they are published;
  • read basic information and performance statistics for the channel's own videos, to decide what to post next.

The application acts only on the channel owner's own YouTube account. It is not offered to the public, does not sign in other users, and does not access anyone else's YouTube data.
