Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

This policy explains what data the ClinchClips application ("the application", "we") accesses, how it is used, stored and shared, and how to revoke access. The application is a private tool used only by the owner of the ClinchClips YouTube channel to manage that channel.

YouTube API Services

The application uses YouTube API Services. By using the application you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's collection and use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we access

When the channel owner authorizes the application with their Google account, it may access, through YouTube API Services:

  • permission to upload videos to the authorized channel and set their title, description, tags and publish time;
  • read-only information about the authorized channel and its own videos, such as video IDs, titles, publish status and copyright status;
  • performance statistics for the channel's own videos, such as views, watch time, likes, comments and subscribers gained.

The application does not access data from any other user's YouTube account, and it does not collect personal information from viewers.

How we use information

Information is used only to operate the ClinchClips channel: to upload and schedule Shorts, to confirm uploads succeeded, to detect copyright claims, and to measure which videos perform well so future posts can be improved.

How we store information

Authorization tokens and channel statistics are stored only on the channel owner's personal computer, in local files and a local database. They are not uploaded to any server we operate. Statistics are kept for as long as they are useful for measuring channel performance and are refreshed or deleted in accordance with the YouTube API Services policies.

How we share information

We do not sell, rent or share information obtained through YouTube API Services with third parties. Summary performance reports are emailed only to the channel owner.

Cookies and device information

The application does not place cookies or similar technologies on any user's device, and it does not collect information from devices other than the channel owner's computer on which it runs. This website does not use cookies or analytics.

Revoking access

You can revoke the application's access to your Google account at any time from the Google security settings page. After access is revoked, the application can no longer upload videos or read data from your channel. To have locally stored data deleted, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
